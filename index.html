Health Coach

Health Coach is a personal health dashboard that the owner of this GitHub account built for his own use. It is not offered to the public, and there is nothing to sign up for.

It runs as a private Google Apps Script web app inside the owner's Google account. With read-only access to his Google Health data from a Fitbit Air, it shows recovery, sleep, activity and long-term health trends, along with rule-based coaching notes that cite their sources.

Health Coach is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition.
